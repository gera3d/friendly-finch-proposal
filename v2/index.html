The Brand Directory · Affiliate Model

You link out. The brand's platform tracks and pays. You reconcile.

A light model by design: no checkout, no cross-site tracking, no chasing payments. It follows your FF-008 specification.

  1. The brand is affiliate-ready. It has an affiliate program, meets your minimum commission and issues a unique Friendly Finch link.

  2. You verify before it goes live. Link tested, disclosure in place, and the terms recorded: rate, window and payment timing.

  3. A Member clicks through. The tracked link sits behind the button, beside a clear commission disclosure. Every click is logged.

  4. The brand's platform does the rest. It attributes the purchase, handles returns, then approves and pays the commission.

  5. You reconcile monthly. Import each platform's statement, match it to clicks and payments, and log any exceptions.
